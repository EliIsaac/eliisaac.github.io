# My Journey as a Chef

I have been a chef for 21 years; this has led to many unique opportunities. From moving to Vancouver from New Brunswick when I was only 20 and had no money to living in most major cities in Canada. Montreal, Toronto, Moncton, Halifax, Victoria among others; Montreal being my favorite.

I even ended up in the Caribbean for a period! Beautiful place, but I don’t suggest living there unless you’re a millionaire!
This has given me a wide variety of skills and experiences, which led to the ownership of my own restaurant for a time. However, it didn’t work out but it gave me valuable career experience. 

## Why I Made the Change

Family loss and health issues have made me reevaluate life and the trajectory I would like it to take. 

Working in hospitality taught me:

- Leadership
- Team management
- Cost Control
- Problem Solving

These skills transfer well into analytics.

## What I am currently studying:

- Excel
- SQL
- Python
- Power BI
- Data Visualization

## Looking Ahead

My goal is to combine my hospitality knowledge and experience with my studies in data analytics to help businesses make better decisions.
